I recently purchased a new clutch master cylinder from eBay. This is the exact unit I ordered: https://www.ebay.com/itm/152584129983 There is still a possibility that the clutch slave cylinder could also be faulty, but I currently believe the slave cylinder is functioning because the master cylinder still technically pumps fuild if manually fed. The main reason I am replacing the clutch master cylinder is because the plastic nipple/feed connection broke off. Since that part is not serviceable or replaceable on its own, I need to replace the entire master cylinder assembly. While I'm waiting, I'm going to search for a olil filter and oil to change that, and soon I'll have to find some coolant for the car as well, then hopefully the car will start moving again.
