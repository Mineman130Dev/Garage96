07/07/26

It’s been a while, but I did get that part changed out, and the car drove perfectly. However, reverse and first gear became increasingly difficult to engage. Come to find out, my Manual Trans Relay Lever Cable Carrier was extremely worn out. This isn’t surprising considering it’s 30-year-old plastic. I ordered a new one and I’m currently in the process of replacing it. After that, I’ll be doing a shifter adjustment, and it should be right back on the road. I did drive it for a bit after replacing the clutch master cylinder. Of course, it’s not legal yet, so I kept it under 45 mph and stayed on back roads, but it drove so nicely and shifted great (when I matched my revs correctly, of course — I’m still learning manual after all 🤷‍♂️). As of this post, I’m in the process of creating a YouTube channel, maybe starting a TikTok, and updating the pictures on the website.

05/15/26

I recently purchased a new clutch master cylinder from eBay. This is the exact unit I ordered: https://www.ebay.com/itm/152584129983 There is still a possibility that the clutch slave cylinder could also be faulty, but I currently believe the slave cylinder is functioning because the master cylinder still technically pumps fuild if manually fed. The main reason I am replacing the clutch master cylinder is because the plastic nipple/feed connection broke off. Since that part is not serviceable or replaceable on its own, I need to replace the entire master cylinder assembly. While I'm waiting, I'm going to search for a olil filter and oil to change that, and soon I'll have to find some coolant for the car as well, then hopefully the car will start moving again.
